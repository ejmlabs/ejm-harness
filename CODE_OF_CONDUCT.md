# Code of conduct

Be respectful, clear, and patient. Discuss the work and support claims with evidence. Welcome questions and give useful feedback. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, reveal private information, or send unwanted sexual content. Do not spam issues or PRs, and do not push or relabel only to start CI runs.

This policy applies in repository issues, PRs, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or block accounts based on severity and repeated behavior. Report a concern privately to a repository maintainer through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Do not post private evidence in a public thread. A maintainer involved in a report should ask another maintainer to handle it. Reports and appeals will be considered in good faith with care for the people involved.
